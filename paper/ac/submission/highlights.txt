Faster than pgSphere at every cone radius from 1 arcsec to 3 deg, real Gaia DR3 too
Cross-matches 20-33% faster than q3c_join, from an index a third of pgSphere's size
Speaks ADQL; one column holds circles and polygons, read and written as IVOA STC-S
